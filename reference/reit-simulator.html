How the numbers are worked out
  • The lumpsum and SIP are totals, split across REITs by your allocation (renormalised to 100% over the REITs switched on). The lumpsum is invested in month 0 and the SIP at the start of each month of the contribution window. Payouts arrive every quarter on units held before the quarter ends. Fractional units are allowed.
  • Each payout is split into interest (taxed at your rate when received), dividend (exempt unless ticked) and return of capital (not taxed, but it lowers your cost; anything beyond your cost is taxed at your rate).
  • Payouts up to and including the last quarter of the window follow the in-window rule (withdraw for 1, reinvest for 2 and 3). Later payouts are split by the harvesting percentage in 2 and 3. Strategy 1 keeps holding after the window with no new money and takes every payout as cash.
  • Off-ramp test (strategy 3): after each quarterly payout, the post-tax payout across all REITs divided by 3 is compared with your total monthly SIP, step-up included. Once it is equal or higher, the SIP stops for the rest of the window and does not restart.
  • Allocation presets: "Tilt to net yield" ranks REITs by post-tax yield at your tax settings and "Tilt to price growth" ranks them by model price growth (or by price CAGR since listing if every REIT shares the same model growth). Weights follow rank: with five REITs, 33%, 27%, 20%, 13% and 7%. Ties share the average.
  • Rental comparison: rent collected for (12 − vacancy) months, less property tax and landlord maintenance; tax at your rate on 70% of (rent less property tax), per the 30% standard deduction. It compares income only; property price growth, loans and exit costs are left to the full comparator.
  • "Net result" is the value if sold after gains tax, plus all cash you took, minus all money you put in, each converted to today's rupees at the date it happened.
  • "If sold" applies capital-gains tax lot by lot: units held over 12 months at the long-term rate after the exemption, the rest at the short-term rate, plus cess. Short-term losses offset long-term gains.
  • Today's rupees divide each year's figures by (1 + inflation) raised to the number of years. Monthly figures are that year's totals divided by 12.
  • Default data, collected September 2026: last two quarterly payouts (Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27) and recent unit prices. Where a Q4 FY26 component split wasn't published, the nearest available split was used. Bagmane Prime Office REIT (listed May 2026) is not included for lack of payout history; add it as a row when you have figures.
